Admission

Rules and Regulations

  • Students must meet the specified age criteria for the desired program.
  • All required documents must be submitted along with the application form.
  • Admission is based on availability and merit.
  • The academy reserves the right to modify admission procedures.
  • Any false information provided will result in immediate cancellation of admission.

Documents and Details Required

Please provide the following documents and details when filling up the admission form:

  • Student's Full Name
  • Date of Birth
  • Gender
  • Nationality
  • Parent/Guardian's Name
  • Contact Number
  • Email Address
  • Permanent Address
  • Present Address (if different)
  • Aadhar Card Number
  • Previous School Name (if applicable)
  • Class/Program Applying For
  • Passport size photograph
  • Birth certificate
  • Previous academic records (if applicable)
  • Any other relevant documents
